Support

Proofpad is a calisthenics workout log for iPhone. If something is wrong, or you have a question, this is where to bring it.

Reach us

Email support@proofpad.co. If it is about your account, include your Proofpad username. We reply by email.

Delete your data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. It is immediate and complete. If you no longer have the app, email us with your username and we will delete the account and everything in it. The privacy policy lists exactly what is erased.
